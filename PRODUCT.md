# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ComfyUI users building image-generation workflows who want small, dependable
utility nodes without adopting a larger workflow framework.

## Product Purpose

Provide explicit ComfyUI nodes for common sizing, image, saving, and model
loading tasks. Success means each node is understandable in the graph, preserves
workflow data, and behaves like a focused extension of ComfyUI.

## Brand Personality

Practical, compact, and dependable.

## Anti-references

Do not imitate oversized custom-node suites, decorative dashboard UI, or
rgthree-specific services beyond the familiar interaction being solved.

## Design Principles

- Make important values and actions obvious in the node.
- Follow ComfyUI's native theme and interaction vocabulary.
- Keep controls compact without hiding meaning.
- Add only the UI required by the workflow task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use theme-derived colors, text labels in addition to position or color, clear
pointer targets, and no decorative motion. Match the accessibility baseline of
the host ComfyUI canvas.
